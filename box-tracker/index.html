Box pace takes the set's per-pack rate times the full box, then subtracts what you've already pulled. Pack odds ignores your pulls and asks what the chance is of at least one more across the packs still sealed.

Every percentage is TCGplayer's published average for that set. Averages hold across thousands of packs, not inside any one box: roughly 1 box in 7 runs hot and 1 in 7 runs cold. Read both columns as the expected shape of a break, not a forecast of yours.
